## Key risks

The stop itself is the first one. An oversold market with a divergence at the low is exactly the setup that gaps *up*, and a 76,240.0 stop filled on a wick before a reversal is the expensive version of being careful. It is accepted deliberately: the overnight is a gap, not a view, and a resting order is the only protection that works while nobody is watching.

Second, the ETF number is incomplete. Minus 258.2 million without IBIT could finalise materially worse, and a headline print tomorrow morning would land on a market that has already lost its daily EMA20.

Third, the daily frame is still intact and the bear case here is entirely short-term. Price is thousands of points above the daily EMA50, EMA100 and EMA200 with the stack in bullish order. Reading a single broken 20-period average as a trend change is how a position trader talks himself out of a good long-term entry, which is why only a fifth of the core was sold.
