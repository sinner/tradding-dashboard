What redeems the session is that end of day named this risk itself. It wrote that a 1.46 percent open-interest drop on a flat tape could be positions squaring ahead of the Tuesday ETF reopen rather than a real unwind, and that the 24.66 USDT held back was the only reason being wrong would not matter much. Both halves of that turned out to be true.

## Key risks

The obvious one is that the flush is not a bottom. Open interest rising 1.25 percent through a 528,247 USD long liquidation event means the accounts being cleared are being replaced faster than they are being removed, and a trapdoor that reloads eventually opens wider. If 78,000 goes on a close, the next measured support is a thousand points away.

The second is that the macro is now driving. Brent up 1.61 percent on US–Iran escalation is a risk-off input that crypto does not control and cannot fade, and headlines out of the Strait of Hormuz do not respect hourly closes.

The third cuts the other way. Today is the first day in five with a live ETF creation channel. If the Tuesday Farside print lands strongly positive into a book this short-term oversold, the 79,000 reclaim could arrive faster than the gate implies, and holding 19.66 USDT of cash back will have been the expensive choice rather than the safe one. That is a risk this desk is willing to carry, because being early with a fifth of the powder costs a fraction of what being wrong with all of it does.
