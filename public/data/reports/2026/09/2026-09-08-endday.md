Rates second: oil at 100 and a 10-year threatening 4.80 are the same trade, and BTC has spent five sessions making lower highs underneath it. Fourth, compression cuts both ways: 15-minute ATR down 33.7 percent and hourly down 13.8 percent into a live catalyst is a coiled spring, and the resolution is more likely to be a gap than a grind. Finally, the daily trend is still intact and well above every daily EMA — a bearish tilt inside a range is not a short signal, and treating it as one is how the last four sessions were lost.
