## Bias review

The 81,500.0 add trigger armed on the 19:00 UTC hourly close at 81,768.9, price ran 516.1 points further to 82,285.0, and it now marks 485.0 points below the trigger. A breakout gate placed under the level that has to break is not a breakout gate — it arms into the last thrust of a move already running. Tonight's add trigger is set 15 points *above* the high for exactly that reason.

The reduce side held clean. The 80,000.0 gate was never approached; the low since midday was 80,896.8, leaving 896.8 points of clearance. The 12 percent core trim at 81,051.0 sold 232.9 points early, roughly six cents on the size involved — a rounding error, not a lesson.
