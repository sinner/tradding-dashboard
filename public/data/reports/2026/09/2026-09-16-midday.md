This session can verify one of the three.

The liquidation leg did not merely hold, it accelerated. Over 09:04 to 12:43 COT the OKX tape carried out 382,235 USD of longs against 28,431 USD of shorts, a 13.4-to-1 skew against the morning's 7.4-to-1. The other two legs are unreadable: both OKX endpoints served cache, open interest 17.4 hours behind the clock on one call and ten days behind on another, the account ratio 12.8 hours behind on the hourly period and two days behind on the five-minute one. They were discarded rather than quoted, which is why `openInterestUsd` and `skew` are null in the JSON rather than filled with plausible-looking numbers.

So the rule is untested at its seventh test. Not passed, not failed. And 74,955.5 remains explicitly not established as a flush low. The endday session inherits an unfinished check plus whatever the Fed does to it.
