## Key risks

The break happened on a Sunday, in the thinnest liquidity of the week, with no ETF flow and no spot bid behind it. That cuts both ways: thin tapes exaggerate moves in the direction they are already going, and they also reverse them cheaply when Monday's real volume arrives. The bullish divergence is a 15-minute signal against a broken daily EMA20, and it should be sized like one.
