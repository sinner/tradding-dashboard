## Key risks

The dominant risk is the one the wallet is deliberately underweighting: open interest that keeps rising into falling price. Three sessions of that is not noise. If the 79,000 cohort gets flushed a second time with the book as thin as a holiday book is, the move through 78,650 will be faster than the levels suggest, because there is no ETF desk on the other side of it until Tuesday.

The mirror risk is being too cautious. The daily chart has not broken anything, RSI is 64, and every session this week that has been sold has been bought back. A holiday tape with no institutional flow cuts both ways, and a squeeze in a market with no short leverage to fuel it would have to come from spot demand — thinner, but not impossible.

Finally, the calibration risk. Two sessions of correct levels and early stances is a pattern that punishes conviction. If the third one comes in the same shape, the right response is smaller size, not a different model.
