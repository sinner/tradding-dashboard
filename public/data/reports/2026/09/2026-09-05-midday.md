## Bias review

The morning session got this one right, and it got it right in a way worth naming. It closed the overnight short hedge, then explicitly refused to flip long — the reasoning being that 79,773.7 had already failed once that morning and open interest was bleeding while price went nowhere, so there was no edge for leverage in either direction. Instead it named a condition: a settled 1H close above 79,905, with the released margin parked in cash to pay for it.

The condition was met. The 16:00 UTC hourly candle settled at 80,042.0, a full 137 points through the gate, and price has held above it for the ninety minutes since. The reduce gate at 79,430 never came close — the session low was 79,546.8, leaving 116.8 points of clearance, and the lowest settled hourly close was 79,615.4.

Both gates correct, in opposite directions, on the same session. That is six consecutive sessions in which a settled-close trigger placed roughly half an hourly ATR beyond a proven wick has resolved correctly, so the spacing stays unchanged again.

The instructive part is not that the gate fired. It is that flipping long at 07:05 would have made more money through worse process. The same discipline that said "do not buy a level that just failed" is what left the cash available when the real signal arrived.
