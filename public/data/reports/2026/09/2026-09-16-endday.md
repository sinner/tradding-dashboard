## What changed

Bitcoin went up anyway, and the reason is sitting in the oil and rates tape rather than in the statement. The ten-year fell back to 4.996 percent from the 5.04 it printed before the announcement. Brent November fell 0.87 percent to 104.91, a long way under the 109.21 the midday session was working with — the Saudi pipeline premium that drove this whole September drawdown is coming out of the price. Hiking into a fading supply shock reads as the end of the repricing rather than the start of one.

Equities did not agree. QQQ traded 711.88 before the statement and closed 704.72, a token 0.18 points higher; VOO closed down 0.43 percent and is the lowest of its trailing 21 sessions. Bitcoin held its post-Fed gains and the Nasdaq did not, and that divergence is the reason this report keeps a range call rather than going outright bullish.
