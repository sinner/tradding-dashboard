## What changed

One thing, and it is the thing that has been blocking every add all week.

Midnight, morning and midday all wrote the same diagnosis in different words. Open interest rising into every decline. The OKX hourly long/short account ratio walking 1.11 to 1.17. Roughly 2.5 million dollars of long leverage forcibly closed across two seven-figure flushes, with the book more long afterward than before. Leverage stacking on leverage into weakness, the trapdoor reloading each time it fired.

Tonight that inverted. Open interest fell 1.46 percent, from 2,186,965,596 to 2,155,023,434 USD, while price moved twenty-one points. That is 31.9 million dollars of notional leaving the book with no price move to explain it — the first decline in four sessions after a run of 2.115bn, 2.131bn, 2.187bn. Funding halved from +0.0000481027 to +0.0000287363 per period. The account ratio eased to 1.16. And the composition of the liquidation flow rotated hard: 186,036 USD of the 656,349 USD liquidated in the rolling window was short-side, 28.3 percent against 2.7 percent at midday.

The distinction that matters is between forced and voluntary. Leverage that gets flushed out by a move comes back, because the same crowd returns for the level that beat it. Leverage that walks out on a flat tape does not.
