The 08:00Z hour opened 78,629.0 and closed 77,878.9 — 271.1 points under the level — and the 09:00Z hour closed 77,908.0 with no reclaim, satisfying the confirmation. The add gate never came close: the session high was 78,803.3, 598.1 points short. And the invalidation held, at 77,750.0, by fifty points.

Did acting help? Marginally not. Spot sits 110.0 points above the confirmation close, so a desk that dumped everything at 77,908.0 is 0.14 percent worse off. That is *acting_helped* false and it makes ten graded sessions running where firing a gate has not paid — though this one failed by 110 points rather than the 1,066 the 31 August morning gate cost, which is a different kind of miss.

The more useful finding is about placement rather than width. Midnight had already widened the reduce level twice, ending at 78,150.0 — 636.6 points and 1.57 hourly ATRs under spot, sat beneath a low that had been tested and defended at 78,183.3. It was still run by 400.0 points and then bounced back to within 132 points of itself. Width was not the failure. The gate was standing inside live liquidation flow: the 78,250 and 78,500 buckets held 5,020,818 USD of realized fills between them, sat directly around the level, and both were consumed in the single hour that broke it. A gate placed where positions are actively dying is not a level, it is a queue. So today's rule changes in kind, not degree — the new reduce gate at 77,700.0 sits *below* the deepest live bucket at 77,750.0, on a floor defended twice in twenty-four hours, with the 4H EMA50 at 77,635.5 as confluence underneath.
