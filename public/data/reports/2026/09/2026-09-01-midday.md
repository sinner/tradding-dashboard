## Key risks

The oil shock is the live variable and it is not a crypto story. Brent up 4.4 percent in a session drags yields, the dollar and equity risk with it, and BTC is trading as the high-beta expression of all three. If crude reverses, most of today's move reverses with it.

Falling open interest cuts both ways. It means no crowded short to squeeze, which caps the bounce case — but it also means the forced selling is running out of sellers. One-sided liquidation flow this extreme is often near-term exhaustion, not the middle of a move.

The 77,320.0 low has held four times. A fifth test that fails opens 77,000.0 quickly, and the 16:00Z hour already proved this tape can run a level 632 points away inside a single bar. Intraday distance in ATRs understates real risk on a day like this.
