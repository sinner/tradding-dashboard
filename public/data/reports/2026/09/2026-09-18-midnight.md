## Key risks

The obvious one is mean reversion: two timeframes at RSI 86-plus, with the forced-buying that produced the move already measurably fading. A 1,000-point retrace from here would be ordinary, not a thesis break.

The second is leverage. Open interest up 5.78 percent against price up 4.83 percent means some of this is borrowed conviction, and borrowed conviction unwinds faster than it accumulates — particularly with a long-liquidation shelf already marked at 79,906.8.

The third is macro, and it is the one that would actually matter. BTC has rallied into a 25bp hike, a 10-year back at 5.00 percent and a dollar up more than a percent on the week. Those are headwinds, not tailwinds, and the rally is currently explained by positioning rather than by the macro picture improving. If tonight's ETF print comes in negative, the flow story that gave this move its footing disappears and what remains is a squeeze with spent fuel above a leverage build.

Finally, a data risk worth naming: the feeds behind this desk returned internally inconsistent timestamps across a 13.7-hour spread today. Every number here is stamped, and the ones that could not be verified are null rather than filled.
