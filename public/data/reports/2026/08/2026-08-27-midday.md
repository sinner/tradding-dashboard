## Bias review

Morning got the gates right and the story wrong, and it is worth separating the two because only one of them cost anything.

The gates were placed well. The ADD gate wanted a settled 1H close above 79,640.7; the 14:00Z bar closed 80,206.2 and cleared it by 565.5 points, taking the second-stage 80,003.4 filter on the same candle. The REDUCE gate at a settled close below 79,250.0 did not fire, but only by 26.7 points — the 12:00Z bar wicked all the way to 78,907.4 and then closed 79,276.7, so the two-bar reclaim clause was never even tested. That is three consecutive sessions where a gate written inside the 0.4-to-0.6 hourly-ATR band resolved inside the session. The placement rule has stopped being an observation and become a rule.

The story is where it went wrong. Morning saw open interest fall 1.63 percent while price rose, concluded the rally was spent short covering, wrote that "above 80,523.5 there is no mapped fuel at all", and acted on it — a TAKE_PROFIT box and a trim of roughly 22.7 USDT out of the spot core at 79,370.0. Price then added another 1,115.8 points, printed 80,850.0, and the market laid down two fresh short-side liquidation rungs at 80,500 and 80,750 in exactly the zone morning had declared empty. Open interest reversed to +1.67 percent. Funding flipped negative. `acting_helped` is false.

The cost is trivial — about 0.32 USDT of forfeited upside, against a 1.01 USDT realized gain that is the first this book has ever booked. The lesson is not trivial. A falling open-interest print during an impulse is a snapshot, not a verdict. Short covering is very often the first leg of a move that new money then joins, which is precisely what happened here between 12:04Z and 17:45Z. The amendment adopted today: require two consecutive same-direction OI readings before letting open interest override a clean multi-timeframe trend signal, and never let one OI print downgrade a box from ADD to TAKE_PROFIT while the 1H frame sits above all four EMAs in ascending order with a positive histogram — which it was, all morning.
