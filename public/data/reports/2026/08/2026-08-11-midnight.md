The long/short account ratio **stopped climbing**. It printed 1.63 at 04:00Z and then 1.62 at 05:00Z, 06:00Z and 07:00Z — four flat hours after three straight sessions of accounts piling into longs while the market fell. The crowd is still heavily long, which is a vulnerability, but it has stopped getting heavier.

## Key risks

A second consecutive ETF outflow on Tuesday's tape is the single thing that would turn a range into a trend. Monday alone is noise; two in a row removes the mechanism that has held the higher-lows structure together since 5 August.

Rising open interest cuts both ways. Fresh positions opened into a box mean the eventual break has more fuel behind it, in whichever direction it goes, and the crowd being 1.62-to-1 long says which side is more exposed.

The 4H EMA200 has held three times, and a level that keeps getting tested usually breaks eventually. Three successful defences build no immunity — they build a cluster of stops just underneath.

Brent up 1.63% with the Strait of Hormuz in the headlines is a live geopolitical tape that can gap any market overnight, and crypto has spent three sessions demonstrating it takes that story harder than equities do.
