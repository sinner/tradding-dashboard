## Bias review

The midnight session called range with a bullish tilt at 34/45/21 from 64,316.4, moved the position to ADD, and did one thing that turned out to matter more than the rest: it cut its add trigger from 65,030 to 64,650. The reasoning at the time was that 65,030 had rejected six times in 102 hours and had stopped being a trigger — waiting for it meant sitting out every repair. Price took 64,650 on the 09:00Z hourly close and then took 65,030 as well, inside the same two hours. That is the best-calibrated call this desk has made this week, and the calibration row backfills accordingly: reduce did not fire, add fired, acting helped, price_next_report 65,020.

There is a lesson in it that is less flattering. The 65,030 level the midnight session retired as unreachable was reached four hours later. Stepping the trigger down was still correct on the evidence available at 02:00 COT — it is the only reason the desk was positioned at all — but this is the second time this week a level got written off shortly before it printed. A level that rejects repeatedly is evidence of compression, not of a ceiling, and compression resolves.
