Morning's action is marked no. It trimmed a fifth of the spot book at 62,874.9 and spot is 170.1 points higher now, so the sold notional is offside by about four cents. The trim was right for two hours and wrong after that.

Eleven sessions in a row the levels have been accurate and the trades have not. Today the mechanism finally became legible. The desk places its exit at the level, and the level is where the reversal happens, because that is where the forced sellers are clustered. A liquidation shelf is by construction the place where selling ends. Placing an exit on top of one is placing an exit on the turn.
