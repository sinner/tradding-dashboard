## Bias review

The midday report set a REDUCE gate at a settled 1H close below 78,200.0 and an ADD gate above 79,000.0. For the first time in three sessions the reduce gate did **not** fire — the session low was 78,402.1, a comfortable 202-point margin. That matters, because the morning's narrower gate at 77,950.0 had fired on a 234-point overshoot and then watched price rally 1,163 points. The midday gate was the first one this desk built deliberately outside the 0.4-to-0.6 ATR band where nine separate whipsaws have now been logged, and it held. The fix works.

The add gate is the other story. It fired cleanly on the 18:00Z close of 79,013.1. Price extended 243 points to 79,256.0 — and gave all of it back. Spot is now 56.0 points *below* the midday tranche at 78,862.6. So the session grades as another loss, but it is not the same loss as the morning's. The morning failed on placement: the gate sat in the noise and the noise triggered it. Midday failed on follow-through: the gate was correctly placed, the signal was real, and the market simply did not extend.

That makes three add-gates in a row — 78,300 at midnight, 78,800 at morning, 79,000 at midday — that confirmed properly and did not pay. Every one pointed at a round number with a short-liquidation cluster immediately above it that the move then consumed on the way through, leaving nothing to sell into. Confirmation proves a move is real. It says nothing about how much room is left. Tonight's add gate is therefore set at **79,256.0**, the price that actually rejected the market, rather than at the next tidy number.

Retail has sold every single leg of this rally, and the rally has been financed by short covering and spot demand rather than fresh leverage. That is the durable kind.

Three things stop this being a bullish report. Daily RSI has crossed into overbought at 70.82, the highest print of the move. The 79,256.0 push failed and the 15m MACD has rolled negative at -20.19. And the macro tape got no friendlier — Brent at 91.09 has added roughly 3.2 percent in two sessions, and US10Y backed up another 1.4bp to 4.772 percent. A real upgrade signal arriving alongside an overbought daily and a failed session high is a wash, not a green light. Confidence stays at 6.0.

Five things sit in it, and the ETF print is the largest: an incomplete row with IBIT missing is an unknown that can move the tape in either direction, and a second consecutive outflow after Friday's -201.9m would be the bearish catalyst with the clearest path into a thin book. A resting stop on a long-term DCA stack would convert a paper drawdown into a realized loss at the worst hour of the day, which is the opposite of what the stack is for.
