## What changed

The US July payrolls report landed at 07:30 COT, twenty-five minutes after the morning report was stamped, and it was a genuine miss. The economy shed 23,000 jobs against a consensus near +83,000, the first outright decline in months. The unemployment rate fell to 4.1%, but only because participation dropped to 61.4%, a five-year low, and average hourly earnings decelerated to +3.2% year on year, the softest reading since May 2021. That is the dovish outcome, and it killed the September hike the market had been pricing.

BTC took it exactly the way you would expect — for about three hours. From 65,020 it ran to 65,388 by 15:00Z, a fresh high for the week. Then it gave all of it back and more, and it did so mechanically. OKX filled **$543,344 of long liquidations against $37,605 of shorts** between 14:01Z and 17:35Z: 122 of 128 fills are `posSide long, side sell`. The session low is 64,519.2, printed at 17:30Z.

Six hours earlier the same tape showed $1,126,392 of *shorts* bought back against zero dollars of longs. Both sides of the leveraged book have now been cleared out inside a single trading day, and price finished the round trip 305 points below where it started it. A book that gets flushed in both directions in twelve hours is a book with no positioning conviction, and that is the main reason this report will not carry a directional confidence above 6.
