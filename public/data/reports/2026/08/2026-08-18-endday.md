## Key risks

The equity handoff is the big one. BTC declined to follow a 1.7 percent Nasdaq decline today, and unfollowed correlation is a debt usually collected in the Asia session. If long-bond yields keep pressing, the collection happens overnight.

The vacuum below is the second. Thin books do not cushion, they gap, and there is nothing measured between 64,500 and 64,042.2. The realistic bad case is a 300-to-500-point drift into that low on nobody's volume rather than a cascade — but the resting stop is sized for exactly that scenario, which is why it is not optional.

The third risk cuts the other way and deserves naming honestly. Two consecutive positive ETF days, falling open interest, de-crowded longs and a held daily ema50 reclaim are a constructive combination, and a trim into that can look foolish by 02:00. It is a deliberate trade of a little upside for cash the desk has said four times it wants and never had.
