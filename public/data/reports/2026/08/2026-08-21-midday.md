## Key risks

The overbought readings are the obvious one. A 4H RSI of 94.31 and a daily RSI of 85.59 do not have to resolve through a decline — they can resolve through time — but they remove any margin for a bad catalyst.

The empty map above spot is the second. A break through 78,258.6 has nothing to feed on, so it needs real spot buying to sustain, and if that buying does not appear the failed breakout is a cleaner short setup than anything available today.

The hole below 75,400 is the third. There is roughly 1,600 points of thin liquidity between there and 73,830, which is where a loss of the invalidation would accelerate rather than drift.

And the fourth is the bond market. The 10-year at 4.740 percent and climbing is the one macro variable that could turn a crypto-flow event into a general de-risking, and the ETF flow that has funded this leg is exactly the kind of money that reacts to it.
