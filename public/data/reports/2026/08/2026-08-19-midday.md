## Key risks

The move is vertical, news-driven and unretested — three properties that historically produce a retracement of half the candle before anything durable forms. Half of today's 5,509.4-point range is 66,970. Open interest barely moved, which is constructive for froth but means there is no trapped long to force a bid on the way down. The Fear & Greed index at 46 has not repriced today at all, so sentiment data on this dashboard is a day stale by construction. And the regulatory catalyst is a *proposal*, not a rule — proposals go to comment periods, get amended, and occasionally die, so some of what was priced in fifteen minutes will be re-litigated over months.
