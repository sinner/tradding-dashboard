Three things worth carrying out of this. Midnight set the reduce 600.7 points wide — 2.54 hourly ATRs on a collapsed ATR of 236.57 — and argued explicitly that when ATR compresses to an extreme, the usual 0.4-to-0.6 band gets too tight to be useful. That reasoning was right. The band would have put the gate 95 to 142 points away, inside a single bar's noise, and it would have triggered during the ordinary afternoon chop hours before the real break happened. Widening a gate when ATR collapses is now a validated adjustment rather than a one-off.

The second is harder. A compressed range warns you that a violent move is coming; it says nothing about direction. Price was above all four EMAs on all four timeframes when the coil broke, which is about as unanimous as a bullish alignment gets, and the release still ran down. The EMA stack told this desk nothing useful about which way the resolution would go.

The third is the one that stings. The strongest bear input was not technical and was sitting there unread. Midnight named the missing 28 August ETF print as the single most important gap in its report, told this session to retrieve it first — and then added anyway. A report that leans bullish on a nine-session inflow streak while unable to read the newest print of that streak is leaning on a number it does not have.

There is very little trapped short interest to squeeze. The 78,100 pocket is mostly one 82-contract fill at the exact 78,160 bounce high — a hint that the bounce ran out of fuel precisely where the last trapped shorts were cleared.

The honest caveat: these are *realized* fills, not resting orders. They mark where leverage has been cleared, not where it is still waiting. That makes them a good map of where stops clustered and a decent proxy for where the next ones sit, but it is not the forward heatmap a keyed CoinGlass feed would give. It cuts both ways — the weak hands in the 77,000–77,500 corridor are gone, but the market now knows the level is reachable.

Open interest is down 6.98 percent from midnight while price fell 1.24 percent. That is deleveraging, not fresh conviction on either side. Funding is still positive at 0.0000669 per 8h with the perpetual trading under the index, which is the signature of longs who have not fully capitulated.
