## Key risks

The clearest one is written into the open interest. This leg was paid for by shorts covering, not by longs arriving — OI down 0.87% against price up 0.24% — and a covering rally ends when the shorts run out. The realized book is only $380,000, which is not a large short position to exhaust. If price pushes through 65,388 and open interest does not turn up with it, the move has no fuel behind it.

Third, the 4H momentum contraction. Two consecutive sessions of a shrinking histogram while price makes new highs is the shape trends take before they stall, and the 20:00Z close is where it either resolves or confirms.

Fourth, liquidity. Volume market-wide is down 52% and the ETF bid does not return for thirty-six hours. Thin tapes move further on less, in whichever direction they choose.
