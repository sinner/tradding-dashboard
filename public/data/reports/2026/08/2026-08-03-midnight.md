## Key risks

The obvious one is that this is a knife-catch dressed as a DCA. Price has made a lower high and a lower low on every timeframe below the daily, the 1H MACD is accelerating away from zero rather than converging, and buying the third test of a level is materially worse odds than buying the first.

The second is the Monday creation window. If the 31 July outflow extends, it hits a market with no short base left to squeeze and a support that has already been leaned on three times.

The third is that the 4H EMA20 and EMA200 are compressed 30 points apart. That compression is what made yesterday's add trigger fire on noise, and the same compression means a bounce into 63,250 will look like a reclaim without being one. The lesson recorded this session is exactly that: check the separation between EMAs before treating a cross as confirmation.
