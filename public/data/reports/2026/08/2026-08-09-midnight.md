What should not be drawn from that is a general licence to act ahead of triggers. The 08 Aug midday's failed breakout buy sits two rows above in the calibration file as the counter-example. The endday acted on a completed momentum cross plus a documented flip in realized-liquidation composition, which is evidence about who is being forced to transact. Level signals on a thin weekend tape have failed twice this week; flow signals have worked twice. That is a sample of four and it is recorded as one.
