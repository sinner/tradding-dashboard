# BTC Morning Analysis — 12 August 2026

**Bias: range, tilted bullish · Confidence 5.5 / 10 · BTC-USDT 64,233.8**

## Bias review

Midnight ended on a question and the tape answered it. The desk had written that the 4H bar opening at 04:00Z was "the single most important print in the morning session's inbox," because price at 63,736.7 was sitting 6.4 points below the 4H EMA200 and threatening to hand back a reclaim it had just confirmed. That bar settled at 63,833.3, ninety points above the average. The next one, opening at 08:00Z, settled 64,236.3. Two consecutive settles above the EMA200, the second expanding by 403 points, is not a level being defended — it is a level being left behind.

Midnight's other two lines behaved as well. The reduce at 63,500 was never approached; the lowest hourly close in the window was 63,797.7 and the lowest wick 63,641.3. The add at 63,930 fired, and it fired on exactly the discrimination it was built for. More on that below, because it is the most useful thing that happened this morning.

## What changed

Price is up 497.1 points, or 0.78%, since the midnight mark. Five hours, five higher hourly highs, and the deepest pullback on the way was 44.8 points.

What matters more than the distance is who paid for it. Realized short liquidations have gone from $828,738 to $1,419,973 — $591,235 of shorts flushed in 4.7 hours — while the long-side total moved $2,037, which is nothing. Funding has fallen from +0.00702% to +0.00384% per eight hours. The long/short account ratio has eased from 1.77 to 1.68. Open interest is up only 0.79% to $2.063bn. Put those together and you get a rally funded by trapped shorts on a book that is becoming less crowded, with longs paying almost nothing to hold. That is the constructive version of this move, and it is a genuinely different animal from the one the desk was worried about last night.

Two things stop this being a louder call. The 15-minute RSI is 72.41, which is the highest reading on that chart in a week and close to the level this desk treats as overbought for crypto. And July CPI prints at 12:30Z — thirty minutes after this report is stamped. Consensus is +0.1% month-on-month, 3.4% headline, core 2.5% against 2.6% prior, with positioning split between 2.4% and 2.5%. Everything here describes a market that has repaired its structure and is about to be handed a number that can undo it.

## Liquidation map — where the magnets are

Measured, not estimated. 857 filled liquidation orders pulled from the OKX public endpoint in-browser, spanning a full 24 hours, newest fill four seconds old at read time.

The headline asymmetry now reads $6,923,622 below spot against $295,771 above — 23.4 to one. That number needs a caveat it did not need before, because most of it is an artefact of the 497-point advance moving price above shelves that were overhead five hours ago. The honest reading is directional. Above spot the ladder is nearly empty: $92,731 at 64,400 and $203,040 at 64,500, and nothing else before the 64,650 area. An advance meets very little friction. Below spot the ladder is dense — $882,624 at 63,900, $1,067,270 at 63,700, $1,735,801 at 63,500 — but that mass sits 300 to 700 points away and only becomes a magnet again if 63,900 gives way. That is why the pull is marked balanced rather than down.

The long/short split of the same tape is the more informative number, and it has improved materially: 4.08x long-side dominance against 7.0x at midnight. Usual caveat applies — this is realized data, a map of where leverage has already been flushed, not a forward book of resting orders.
