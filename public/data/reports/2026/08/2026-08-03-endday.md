It rested on a 4H EMA200 of 63,247.50 computed over a 250-bar window seeded at the first close of that window. A 200-period average over 250 bars carries 8.2% of its weight in the seed, and that seed came from a much higher price regime, dragging the result 740 points too low. Seeded properly, from an equivalent-half-life daily EMA33, the 4H EMA200 is **63,988.10**. Price never touched it. Two things follow: the reduce level moves down to 63,040, anchored to a real object rather than a phantom moving average, and confidence comes down to 5.60.
