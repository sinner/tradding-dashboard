## Bias review

Midnight marked 62,585.2, carried a bearish tilt at 26/40/34, and named 62,284.3 — the 1 August low — as the level that had never been closed through on a 4H basis. Price went straight for it. The 04:45 COT candle printed 62,311.3, which is 27.0 points and 0.043% above that low, and stopped there. It then reclaimed 62,410, reclaimed 62,585 inside thirty minutes, and ran to 62,864.5 by 05:45 before fading back to where it started.

So the level work was right for a fifth consecutive session, and the scalp instruction was right too. Midnight wrote that the tag of the lower magnet "is the event, not the direction" and that a tag which immediately reclaims 62,585 is a long. That is precisely what traded, and it paid 553 points.

What midnight got wrong was where the pattern would fire. It laddered the next add against 61,999.35, the second measured liquidation shelf, and the market executed the same tag-and-reclaim 305 points higher at the structural low. The reserved cash never reached the planned rung. That is the lesson of this session and it is now in the ledger: buyers defend the structural low, stops sit at the liquidation shelf beneath it, so the first rung belongs at the low and only the second at the shelf.

The bearish tilt itself earned nothing. Five hours later spot is 2.6 points below the midnight mark. Immaterial either way.
