## Bias review

Yesterday's morning report called range with a bullish tilt at 33/42/25 and backed it with a small 3x futures long at 63,106.0, stopped at 62,690. The read was right. The execution was not. Price fell to 62,284.3 within three hours, took the stop out at 12:45 COT, and then rallied 2.40% to 63,782.4 with the desk watching from the sideline. A 416-point stop is 0.26 of a 1,619-point daily ATR — well inside the noise this asset generates in an ordinary session. The thesis went on to pay 676 points.

The level work, though, was the best this desk has produced in a week. The report named 62,279.54 as the heaviest single band on the 48-hour liquidation map and specifically flagged that it had never been tagged in this leg. It was tagged. The low printed 62,284.3 — four dollars and ninety cents above the band — and not one 4H candle closed beneath it. The add trigger was equally precise: a 4H close above the 4H EMA200 at 63,269.18. Two consecutive 4H bars closed at 63,451.4 and 63,570.6. Both levels did exactly what they were published to do.

So the honest score is: bias right, reduce level right, add level right, sizing wrong. That is the third straight session where the analysis has outperformed the position.
