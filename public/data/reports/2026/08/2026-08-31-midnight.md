What happened next is the interesting part. Price went straight through the gate — down to 77,000.0 at 04:45Z, four hundred points beneath it — and then reversed hard. The lowest settled hourly close of the entire move was 77,473.7, seventy-three points *above* the reduce level. The gate was pierced on a wick and never confirmed on a close. Both gates tested. Neither fired. A desk trading the raw levels would have sold the low and bought the high inside an hour and a half.
