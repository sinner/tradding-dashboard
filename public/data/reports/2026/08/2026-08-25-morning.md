Four consecutive reports had found that every loss in this book was a trim taken into strength, so midnight inverted the habit and added instead. It lost anyway.

The finding was too narrow. Inverting a bad habit is not the same as fixing it. The four losing trims and this losing add share one feature: entry at the extreme of an extended move. Midnight bought 553 points below a 24h high, six hours into a 1,439.8 point vertical, with a daily RSI of 87.62 and price 14.69 percent above its own daily EMA20. It named all three of those facts and sized down for them rather than waiting for them to work off. The error was the price, not the direction — and the structure it read holds up, since the 78,717.2 invalidation was never breached.

The one clean winner in the eight-session window remains the 24 August seeder's 15 USDT buy at 78,847.4, taken into weakness near an identified support shelf. The rule is about where, not which way.

## Key risks

The daily chart is still 12.70 percent above its own EMA20 with an RSI of 86.35. A 1.94 percent flush resolves almost none of that, and the unwind of an extension this size does not usually happen in one six-hour session.

The long-liquidation shelf below is the specific mechanism to watch. Losing 78,880.0 feeds 8.45m of stops in a 500 point band, and cascades through clusters that one-sided do not stop politely at the first support.

Against that, the reclaim risk is real too. The 15m is the most oversold it has been all advance, funding shows no froth to unwind, and six days of fund inflows have not blinked. A sharp bounce to 79,999.8 would leave this trim looking like the fifth entry made at the wrong end of a swing — which is exactly the failure mode this report just finished diagnosing. Position sizing, not conviction, is the defence against being wrong in either direction here.
