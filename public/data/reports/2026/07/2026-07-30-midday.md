Price is $120 above the sale four and a half hours later, so the round trip on that slice is about 0.19%, and the event it was hedging resolved in the wallet's favour. That is what optionality is supposed to cost.

The miss is the bid. Morning parked the freed cash at $64,250–$64,450 and the market never came within $160 of the top of that band. The session low was 64,608.8. So the read was right and the level was wrong, and 31 USDT sat idle through the most informative six hours of the week. The band was drawn from where the breakout *launched* rather than from where the order flow actually is. This report moves the trigger to where the market traded.

## Key risks

An asset that cannot hold a breakout on a +3% Nasdaq day with the dollar at a six-week low is telling you something about its own bid, and if the equity move fades tonight on Apple or Amazon there is nothing underneath BTC that today's tape has demonstrated. And fourth, Fear & Greed at 28 alongside a five-day range that refuses to break either way is the profile of a market waiting for something, which usually means the eventual move is larger than the range suggests.
