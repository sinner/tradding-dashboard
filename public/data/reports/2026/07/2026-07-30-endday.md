## What changed

Four things, and the first carries the others.

**Non-confirmation.** An asset that will not go up on its best possible news has a thin marginal bid. That is the whole of the bearish tilt.

**The daily EMA50 rejected price twice.** It sits at 64,935.52. The session high of 65,075.0 failed under it and the day high of 65,152.4 failed under it. The completed 2026-07-30 candle closed 64,781.7 — below the gate — and the new candle is below it too.

**The liquidity pull inverted.** Midday read the pull as up, with the heaviest measured cluster $183 above spot. Tonight the heaviest band near price sits *below*.

**Sentiment went the wrong way.** Crypto Fear & Greed printed 25 — Extreme Fear — at 00:00Z, against 28 at midday, on a day equities ripped. Equities are euphoric and crypto is not joining the mood. That is a positioning problem, not a macro one.
